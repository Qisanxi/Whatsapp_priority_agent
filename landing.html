02 / RULES

Four tiers.
Two speeds.

Every inbound message lands in one of four priority classes. Two of them never touch a human queue. Two of them never skip one. That single rule is the whole product — the LLM just enforces it, every second of every day.

classify → generate → store → route

T-01
Urgent INSTANT

Auto-replied the moment it's classified. The customer never sees a wait.

Server down, we're losing money every minute!
T-02
High INSTANT

Real problems with paying customers on the other end — answered by AI within seconds, grounded in conversation history.

Payment gateway is declining every UPI transaction
T-03
Normal HUMAN-IN-LOOP

A draft reply is generated and queued. A human approves, edits or rejects — nothing leaves without a signature.

How do I get a replacement credit card?
T-04
Low HUMAN-IN-LOOP

Praise, small talk, general queries. Acknowledged warmly — by a person, when they have a moment.

Absolutely love your product, great job team!
03 / SYSTEM

One private pipeline.
Zero third-party APIs.

Inference stays on a dedicated vLLM instance in AMD Radeon Cloud (ROCm). Memory, routing and approvals all live in your own stack — the context window is filled from your own Postgres, nothing else.

05 / TRACK 2

Requirement by requirement.

How the build maps to the hackathon's agent checklist — no hand-waving, each capability points at code that ships in the repo.

R-01Local Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)Conversation history persisted in PostgreSQL is assembled into the LLM context window on every single turn.
R-02Tool Invocation/api/webhook/message accepts external triggers — WhatsApp webhooks, cron jobs, CI smoke tests.
R-03Multi-step Task PlanningA fixed four-stage pipeline — classify → generate → store → route — with per-stage state and failure handling.
R-04Local Multi-turn MemoryPer-contact history is maintained across sessions in Postgres and replayed as context, so replies stay coherent.
R-05Permission Control & PrivacyNORMAL and LOW messages are held for human approval — nothing is auto-sent without a rule that says so.
